Questions

Straight answers

We don't have clean data. Can we still start?

Yes. An exported log of timestamps plus interviews and walk-the-process sessions is enough to start.

Will this replace our people?

The method includes "keep human" as a class. The goal is to remove rework and waiting, not to promise headcount changes.

Is AI involved, and how is it controlled?

Yes, in analysis and development. We use only tools you approve in writing, a person reviews every output, your security team approves each pilot, and the use of AI is disclosed in the agreement.

Can you work with our Google tools?

Yes. The pilots are built on the tools you already hold, which may include Google tools, with AI assistants such as Gemini or Claude if you already use them. No new license is used without your written approval.

Why not just hire a build team?

The Program designs before it builds, so the two pilots are the ones worth building, and you keep a plan for what comes next.

How does payment work?

One fixed fee, set in the written agreement for your scope. It is paid in three stages: 30% at kickoff, 30% when the design is approved, and 40% at final delivery.

Is there help after the 12 weeks?

Optionally, a monthly continuous improvement advisory with a set number of hours, agreed in a separate written addendum.
