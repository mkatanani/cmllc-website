Questions

How long does the Diagnostic take?

Three weeks, start to finish, with five deliverables including a live readout with your leadership team.

No. The Diagnostic has no software cost. In a build, licenses and cloud costs are yours, and any chargeable add-on is flagged in writing before you sign.

A dedicated Principal Consultant, the same person at every tier. There is no bench and no hand-off to someone you have not met.

Yes. If you already know what you need, you can start with the monthly advisory or a build. Those begin with a short, condensed current-state step that uses the same rubrics.

The Diagnostic is 50% at signing and 50% at delivery. The advisory is invoiced monthly in advance. Builds are 30% at kickoff, 30% at the midpoint and 40% at final delivery.
